How Discover works

Discover lists new posts from the sites you read, so you don't have to go looking for them. It's kept apart from your extracts: nothing is added to your library until you choose to save it. On iPhone, Discover is a tab at the bottom of the screen. On iPad and Mac, it's in the sidebar.

Which sites you follow

A site is followed once you've saved two extracts from it. Vanilla's own site is followed from the start, which is how this post got here. Tap Sites in Discover to see them all:

  • Turn a site on or off. A site you turn on stays followed however many extracts it has, and one you turn off stays off.
  • Turn off Follow New Sites if you'd rather choose every site yourself.
  • Use Add Feed to follow a site by its address, before you've saved anything from it.

You can also follow a site from any extract of it: tap ⋯ in the reader and choose Follow. The same menu has Unfollow once you follow it, and any post in Discover can stop following its site.

When you share a page to Vanilla and it's the second you've saved from that site, the share sheet tells you it's now following the site.

How it finds a site's posts

Most sites publish a feed: a list of their latest posts in RSS, Atom or JSON Feed. When you save an extract, Vanilla notes the feed the page links to. When a page doesn't link to one, Vanilla tries a few addresses where feeds are usually kept (such as /feed and /rss.xml). If it still finds nothing, it tries that site again a month later.

When it checks

Vanilla checks feeds while the app is open, and in the background now and then while it isn't, so new posts are waiting when you come back. Your device decides when background checks happen, and each one looks at a handful of sites, the ones longest overdue first. You can pull down on Discover to check every site straight away.

Background checks wait for Wi-Fi and skip Low Data Mode. To let them use cellular too, turn off Wi-Fi Only in Sites. Checks while the app is open use any connection.

A site isn't checked every time. Vanilla checks a feed as often as the site tends to post, between every two hours and once a week. It never checks sooner than the site asks. A site that fails to answer is left a while before it's tried again, and a little longer each time.

If you haven't opened, saved or dismissed any of a site's posts in 30 days, Vanilla checks it half as often. Sites says so beside it, in case you'd rather stop following it. Opening one of its posts puts it back to normal.

What it downloads

Only the feeds. Vanilla never downloads the articles in them in the background. An article is fetched only when you open it.

When a feed hasn't changed since the last check, the site can say so without sending it again, so most checks are tiny.

What it lists

Posts from the last week. The first time Vanilla checks a site, it lists that site's three newest posts, and after that up to ten new ones per check, so following a busy site doesn't flood the list. Posts you've already saved or dismissed are left out.

A new post has a dot beside it, and Discover shows how many new posts there are, until you open them.

Reading a post

Opening a post shows it the way your extracts look, with its text and images in the reader, but it isn't saved yet. From there:

  • Save adds it to your extracts, with no second download.
  • Dismiss takes it off the list.
  • Stop Following stops new posts from that site.

You can do the same by swiping a post in the list.

Privacy

Vanilla asks each site for its feed directly from your device. There's no Vanilla server in between, and nobody else learns which sites you follow. The sites you follow and the posts you've dismissed sync between your devices with iCloud. The posts themselves stay on each device and are cleared after 30 days.
