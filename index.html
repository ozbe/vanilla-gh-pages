Vanilla

Save web pages to read later, and see new posts from the sites you already read. Summaries are written on your device, for every page, with no account.

Join the beta on TestFlight
Install TestFlight on your iPhone, iPad or Mac, then open the link there.

Each saved page, an extract, keeps its text on your device to read offline, on a sepia page or in a warm dark mode. Save one with the + button, the share sheet, or Safari, where Vanilla's extension opens the same clean reader right over the page.

  • Discover. New posts from the sites you already read, with no feeds to set up. Vanilla follows the sites you save from, marks promotions so you can hide them, and puts the count of new posts on its icon. On your Apple Watch too. How Discover works.
  • On-device smarts. With Apple Intelligence, every extract gets a short summary, key points, the people and places it mentions, and suggested tags. No monthly limit, and nothing leaves your device.
  • No account. Your library lives on your devices and in your own iCloud, not on our server. There's no sign-up and no tracking: your privacy.
  • Bring your library. Leaving Pocket or Omnivore? Import from them, or from Instapaper, Matter, Readwise Reader, Raindrop, Pinboard or a bookmarks file, with tags and what you've read.
  • Listen. Have an extract read aloud while the text follows along, and highlight the sentence you just heard from the lock screen or with Siri. It plays from your Apple Watch too, with the screen off.
  • Highlights and notes. Highlight text or add a note, as many as you like, free. Find them all again in Highlights, or take them with you when you share an extract as Markdown.
  • Everywhere you read. iPhone, iPad, Mac and Apple Watch, kept in step with iCloud. Plus widgets for your Home Screen and Lock Screen, Siri and Shortcuts.
  • PDFs too. Save or share a PDF and Vanilla turns it into readable text, figures included, scanned pages too.
  • Recipes. A recipe opens on the recipe, not the story above it, with scaling, a cook mode of one step at a time, timers, and a shopping list for Reminders.
