Vanilla

Save web pages and read them later, in a clean reading mode, offline.

Each saved page, an extract, keeps its text on your device and loads its images as you need them. Add a link with the + button, the share sheet or Safari, then search, filter by tag and read or unread, or have an extract read aloud to you.

  • Read in Safari. Vanilla's Safari extension opens a page in the same clean reader, right over it, and adds it to your extracts if you want to keep it.
  • PDFs too. Save a link to a PDF, share one from any app, or drop one on the list on a Mac. Vanilla turns it into readable text, figures included, and reads scanned pages with Vision.
  • Highlights and notes. Select text to highlight it or add a note. Find them all again in Highlights, or take them with you when you share an extract as Markdown.
  • Discover. New posts from the sites you already read, fetched straight from their feeds, with promotions marked so you can hide them. How Discover works.
  • More than text. A recipe gets a cook mode with timers, scaling and a shopping list for Reminders. Extracts also get a contents list, footnotes in place, and the dates, places and links a page mentions.
  • On-device smarts. With Apple Intelligence, Vanilla suggests tags and writes a short summary and key points. It all happens on your device.
  • Bring your library. Import from Pocket, Instapaper, Omnivore, Matter, Readwise Reader, Raindrop, Pinboard or a bookmarks file, with tags and what you've read.
  • Everywhere you read. iPhone, iPad, Mac and Apple Watch, kept in step with iCloud. Plus widgets for your Home Screen and Lock Screen, and Siri.
  • Easy on the eyes. A sepia page, a warm dark mode, and a choice of themes, fonts and sizes.
