Privacy

Vanilla doesn't collect anything about you. There's no account, no analytics, no ads and no tracking, and no Vanilla server.

What Vanilla keeps

Your extracts, tags, highlights and notes, the sites you follow, and the Discover posts you've opened or dismissed. They're kept on your device and, if you're signed in to iCloud, synced between your devices through your own private iCloud database. Apple stores that data under its privacy policy. I can't see it.

Discover's posts stay on each device and are cleared after 30 days.

What happens on your device

Everything Vanilla works out about an extract is worked out on your device: reading the page into an extract, turning a PDF into text, reading scanned pages with Vision, reading aloud, and, where your device has Apple Intelligence, suggested tags, summaries, key points, a PDF's missing title and authors, and which Discover posts are promotions. None of it is sent anywhere.

Importing a library reads the export file you pick on your device.

Who Vanilla talks to

Vanilla only connects to the web to fetch what you've asked for, straight from your device:

  • The pages you save. Vanilla loads the page the way a browser would, so the site sees a visit from your device. While it reads a page, it blocks known ad, verification and analytics services. Images are loaded from the page's site when you read an extract, and each site's icon comes from the site itself. Links you import are fetched the same way, in the background.
  • The feeds of sites you follow, for Discover. How Discover works says when and how often.
  • YouTube and Vimeo, for a video's title and picture, when you look at the videos in an extract that links to them.
  • Apple, for iCloud sync, and Apple Maps if you tap an address in an extract.

The Safari extension

The extension only reads a page when you press its button. It reads that page to show it in Vanilla's reader, and adds it to your extracts only if you choose to.

Permissions

Vanilla asks for access to Reminders only when you send a recipe's shopping list there, and uses it only to add that list.

The beta and feedback

If you're testing Vanilla on TestFlight, Apple shares the crash reports and feedback you choose to send with me, under its privacy policy. If you email me, I use your message and address only to reply.

Removing your data

Delete an extract in the app and it's gone from all your devices. Deleting the app removes what it kept on that device. To remove what's in iCloud, go to Settings, then your name, iCloud, and manage Vanilla's storage.
