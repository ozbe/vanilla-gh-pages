Support

Something not working, or not sure how? Send a note to vanilla@ozbe.dev and I'll get back to you. The answers below may get you there first.

Getting started

  • Save a page. In Safari or any other app, tap Share and choose Vanilla. Or copy a link and tap "+" on the Extracts screen.
  • Read it in Safari. Turn on Vanilla's Safari extension, then use its button to read the page you're on in Vanilla's reader and add it to your extracts.
  • Discover. Vanilla follows the sites you save from and lists their new posts in Discover. You can follow a site yourself with Add Feed.
  • Listen. In the menu at the bottom of an extract, tap Listen and Vanilla reads it to you, highlighting as it goes.
  • Coming from Pocket, Instapaper or Omnivore? Touch and hold "+" and choose Import Library (on an iPad, it's also in the File menu).

The welcome extract covers the rest.

Questions

My extracts aren't showing up on my other devices.

Vanilla syncs through your own iCloud. Check that each device is signed in to the same Apple Account, and that iCloud is on for Vanilla (Settings, then your name, then iCloud). A new device can take a few minutes to bring in a large library. If your iCloud storage is full, new changes stay on the device they were made on until there's space.

I can't find summaries, suggested tags or Ask.

These use Apple Intelligence on your device, so they appear only on an iPhone, iPad or Mac that has it, with Apple Intelligence turned on. Everything else in Vanilla works without it.

How do I turn on the Safari extension?

On an iPhone or iPad, go to Settings, then Apps, Safari, Extensions, and turn on Vanilla Extract. Then, in Safari, tap the page menu in the address bar to find it. The extension only reads a page when you press its button.

Vanilla doesn't offer to translate an extract.

Vanilla offers Translate under the title when an extract is in a language other than yours, and only for languages your device can translate. The first time, your device may ask to download the language. Translations are made on your device and kept on it.

A page came out wrong, or missing part of the article.

Some sites are harder to read than others. Send me the link at vanilla@ozbe.dev and I'll look at it. In the meantime, Share, then Original Link opens the page as it was.
